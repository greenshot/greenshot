Getting started

Greenshot runs in the background. Its icon is next to the clock, in the notification area of the taskbar. Click it with the right mouse button to open the menu with all captures, the Preferences... and this help.

Don't see the icon? Windows 11 may hide it behind the arrow (^). Your setup in Self-Service shows it next to the clock for you, and also checks whether the Print key reaches Greenshot.

Taking a screenshot

Start a capture with a hotkey or from the menu of the Greenshot icon:

The menu also has Capture window from list, which captures an open window you pick without the capture screen, Open image from file and Open last capture location. You can change the hotkeys in the settings, General tab.

The capture screen

Greenshot takes a picture of all your monitors and shows it on top of everything. Nothing changes while you select, so an open menu or a tooltip stays where it was. Only the part you select is captured. Esc closes the capture screen without capturing anything.

On top of the picture you see:

  • the selection with its size,
  • the zoomer, a magnifier next to the mouse which shows every pixel, so you can place a corner exactly,
  • panels in the corners: the keys (F1), the info (I) and the preview of the selection (P). They move out of your way on their own.

Press F1 while selecting: the keys panel always lists the keys which work at that moment. Greenshot remembers when you hide a panel, F1 or I brings it back.

Region, window, fixed size and text

  • Region: drag a rectangle with the mouse, letting go captures it. With the keyboard: the arrow keys move the mouse, Enter starts the selection, Enter again captures it. Hold Shift to change only the width or only the height.
  • Window (Space): the window under the mouse is selected, a click or Enter captures it. Greenshot selects the innermost part, e.g. a toolbar; move to the outer edge of a window to select the whole window. Tab and Shift + Tab select the next or the previous window.
  • Fixed size (F): a rectangle of an exact size, e.g. 1280 × 720, follows the mouse. 1 – 9 pick a size, C changes the corner the mouse holds, hold Alt to place it without snapping to windows. Your own sizes are set in the Capture tab, the sizes of your last captures are offered too.
  • Text (T): Greenshot reads the text on the screen with the text recognition of Windows and frames every line. Click a line, or drag over words, to capture them. To get the words as text, send the capture to Windows OCR, which copies the text to the clipboard. This needs a recognition language installed in Windows; the language is chosen in the Capture tab, Text recognition (OCR).

With the QR code plugin, Greenshot finds QR codes on the screen and frames them. Click one to copy its content or to open its web address.

Selecting without freezing the screen

Turn off Freeze the screen while selecting in the Capture tab, or press L while selecting, and you select on the live screen: a video keeps playing, and the capture is taken when you finish the selection. The text mode and the preview need the frozen picture. Open menus usually close when you click, so use the frozen screen for them.

Something only appears after a moment? Set Milliseconds to wait before capture in the Capture tab, or select on the live screen.

Where the screenshot goes

After the capture, Greenshot sends the screenshot to your destinations. By default it asks every time with a small menu. Every destination there has a fixed underlined letter, e.g. C for the clipboard and S to save, so you can pick it with the keyboard. Choose your destinations in the Destination tab, or in Quick preferences in the menu of the icon.

  • Open in image editor: annotate the screenshot first, see the image editor.
  • Save directly uses the folder and the filename pattern of the Output tab, Save as asks for them.
  • Copy to clipboard, Send to printer, E-Mail, Windows share and Windows OCR (copies the text of the screenshot).
  • Plugins add more destinations, e.g. Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote, PDF, Box, Dropbox, Jira and Confluence. They are set up in the Plugins tab, where you can also switch a plugin off.

The Available column of the Destination tab switches a destination off everywhere. Which notifications you get after a capture is set in the Capture tab, Notifications.

The image editor

The editor adds shapes, text, arrows, highlights and more to a screenshot, or hides what others shouldn't see. Besides your captures it opens images from a file or from the clipboard (menu of the icon). Everything you draw stays an element you can select, move, change or remove.

Tabs

An editor window shows several images as tabs, a new capture opens as a tab in the editor window you used last. Ctrl + Tab goes to the next tab and Ctrl + W closes one. Drag a tab to another editor window, or click it with the right mouse button to move it to a new window. The + on the tab strip starts a new, empty image. When you close a window with unsaved images, Greenshot asks once: Save all, Discard all or cancel. Prefer one window per capture? Switch the tabs off in the Editor tab.

Drawing tools

Choose a tool on the left of the editor, in the Object menu or with its key, then drag on the image. The toolbar at the top shows the options of the tool or of the selected elements, e.g. line color, fill color and thickness.

  • Hold Shift while drawing for a square or a circle, or for lines in steps of 15°; while resizing it keeps the aspect ratio. Hold Ctrl to draw or resize from the middle, handy for an ellipse around something.
  • Text: draw the text box and type. Double click a text to change it, Enter finishes, Shift + Enter starts a new line.
  • Selected elements: the arrow keys move them (with Shift ten pixels), Ctrl + arrow keys resize them, Page Up / Page Down move them up or down a level, Delete removes them, Ctrl + D duplicates them. Hold Shift and click to select several elements.
  • Ctrl + Z undoes, Ctrl + Y redoes.

Highlighting and obfuscating

Draw the highlight (H) or obfuscate (O) area like a rectangle and choose the mode in the toolbar:

  • Highlight text colors the area like a text marker, Highlight area darkens and blurs everything else, Grayscale turns everything else gray, Magnify enlarges the area.
  • Pixelize or Blur hide passwords, names, account numbers or faces.

Cropping

Choose the crop tool (C) and draw the area to keep, Enter confirms, Esc cancels. Press C again, or use Crop mode in the toolbar, to switch the mode:

  • Crop out vertically or Crop out horizontally removes a strip from the image and joins the rest, e.g. to leave out the empty middle of a long page. Cut edges chooses how the seam looks: straight, zig-zag, wavy, torn or seamless.
  • Auto crop removes a border of one color around the image.

Adding images and re-using elements

  • Drag an image file into the editor, or paste an image with Ctrl + V. Insert window in the Edit menu adds a capture of another open window.
  • Use the same elements on many screenshots: Save objects to file in the Object menu, and Load objects from file on the next screenshot.
  • Save as a .greenshot file to keep every element editable and continue later.

Automatic steps and recipes

Recipes do things with your captures automatically, e.g. add a border to every capture, or save and upload it in one go. Quick preferences > Automatic steps in the menu of the icon switches the ready-made steps on or off, More… opens their settings in the Recipes tab. The Recipes menu of the icon starts your recipes and opens the Recipe Manager and the Recipe Editor. Recipes come with the Recipe Editor plugin.

The settings

Open the settings with Preferences... in the menu of the icon, or with the links below.

  • General: language, colors (light, dark or like Windows), icon size, Launch Greenshot on startup, the hotkeys, the proxy and how often Greenshot checks for updates.
  • Capture: capturing the mouse pointer, camera sound, magnifier, freezing the screen, notifications, the wait before a capture, your fixed sizes, interactive window capture, and the language of the text recognition.
  • Output: the folder and the filename pattern for saving (click ? for the placeholders, e.g. ${YYYY}-${MM}-${DD} or ${title}; a \ in the pattern creates folders), the image format and the JPEG quality.
  • Destination: what happens after a capture, see where the screenshot goes.
  • Editor: tabs, the size of the editor window, the question about saving when you close it, and the size and color of a new image.
  • Printer: fit the image to the page, rotate or center it, print in color, grayscale or inverted, and a footer with date and time.
  • Plugins: switch plugins on or off and configure them. Changes apply at the next start of Greenshot.
  • Expert: settings for special cases, e.g. remote desktop, DirectX capture (needed for HDR screens), memory and speed, and the clipboard formats. Only change them when you know what they do.

Greenshot keeps its settings in greenshot.ini, in %APPDATA%\Greenshot (the portable version keeps it next to Greenshot.exe). Files & Configuration in Self-Service shows where it is.

When something doesn't work

Self-Service checks Greenshot and fixes most problems. Open it with About Greenshot in the menu of the icon, then Self-Service, or go straight to the right section:

  • Print does nothing or opens something else: Hotkeys & Conflicts shows whether OneDrive or the Snipping Tool took the key, and gives it back to Greenshot.
  • Can't find your screenshots: Files & Configuration shows where Greenshot saves them.
  • Copying to the clipboard fails: Clipboard Diagnostics finds the program which blocks the clipboard.
  • Greenshot crashes or a plugin is missing after an update: Installation Integrity checks the installed files.
  • Reporting a problem: Copy System Info in System & Hardware and the errors in the log help us to help you. Report problems at github.com/greenshot/greenshot/issues.

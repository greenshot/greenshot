Adds **Apply Confidential Watermark** to the editor's recipe menu. It puts a red CONFIDENTIAL banner and a watermark
on the capture that is open in the editor. Nothing is saved or sent; you keep editing.

## Use it

In the editor, choose **Apply Confidential Watermark** from the recipe menu.

Captures a region you select and sends it to your default printer without opening the print options or printer dialog.

## Use it

- Tray menu: **Region to Printer (silent)**
- Hotkey: Ctrl + Alt + Shift + P, change it in Settings > General or in the recipe editor

## Options

| Option | Default |
|---|---|
| Hotkey | Ctrl + Alt + Shift + P |

## Allows

Sends the capture to the default printer without prompting for printer or print settings.
